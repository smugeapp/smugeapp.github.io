How to use Smudge
  • Paint: pick a brush on the left and a colour on the right.
  • Stickers: drag to move, corner dot to resize, top dot to turn. Copy, paste and flip from the toolbar.
  • Faces: drag a face sticker onto a stick person's head to give them that face, or pick a stick person and tap 😀 Face.
  • Outlines: colouring pictures, letters and numbers. The fill bucket colours inside the lines.
  • Shapes: drag from corner to corner. Hold Shift for perfect circles and squares.
  • Word art, backgrounds and frames are in the right panel.
  • Make a scene and Party mode are under Fun stuff.
  • Replay plays back how your painting was made.
  • Install: on Android or a computer tap “Install app”. On iPhone or iPad tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.
  • Keys: Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy and paste stickers · [ ] size · F hide panels · O outlines
